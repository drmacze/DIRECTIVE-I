01 / THE DIRECTIVE

The city fell.
The mission did not.

DIRECTIVE I is designed as a focused, cinematic apocalypse experience: tactical encounters, hostile infected, scarce resources, and weapons that feel distinct instead of interchangeable.

The presentation stays minimal and grounded—closer to a modern AAA military survival title than a crowded Minecraft mod page.

03 / CORE SYSTEMS

Designed for consequence.

Every system supports the same goal: make each encounter feel readable, deliberate, and dangerous.

01

Weapon identity

Range, recoil, handling, ADS behavior, and attachment trade-offs define how each firearm performs.

02

Ballistics

Projectile behavior and combat feedback are built to feel physical instead of purely cosmetic.

03

Infected pressure

Encounters are staged around threat, visibility, sound, and limited breathing room.

04

Dark-world atmosphere

Minimal UI, controlled color, fog, lighting, and environmental storytelling keep the tone grounded.
