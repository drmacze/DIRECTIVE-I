01 / THE DIRECTIVE

The city fell.
The mission did not.

DIRECTIVE I is a focused cinematic apocalypse experience: tactical encounters, hostile infected, scarce resources, and weapons that feel distinct instead of interchangeable.

The presentation stays minimal and grounded—closer to a modern AAA military survival title than a crowded Minecraft mod page.
