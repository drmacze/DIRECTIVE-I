Main Story / Season I

The Broken Signal

You are not an ordinary survivor. You are a sleeper agent of A.P.E.X, awakened inside the ruins of an underground viral isolation facility with fractured memories, no team, and a directive you no longer understand.

Automated emergency transmission // Sector 4

“Warning. Sector 4 has fallen. To all remaining agents, initiate Directive I.”

Classified / Initial Condition

No memory.
No extraction.

The facility beneath Sector 4 has been torn apart. Its database is corrupted, the chain of command is gone, and the meaning of Directive I has been erased from your access files.

When you reach the surface, the scale of the collapse becomes clear. Cities are shattered, the wilderness is reclaiming abandoned districts, and the infected are not ordinary undead. They are mutations born from a biological weapon.

Season I / Mission Chain

Three acts. One buried truth.

ACT 01

The Aftermath

Escape the underground facility, secure basic weapons, and survive the first infected zones. A crashed military helicopter becomes the first real lead. Its blackbox contains fragments of Directive I and coordinates pointing deeper into Sector 4.

ACT 02

The Trail of Dead

The recovered data leads through abandoned control checkpoints, military sites, research facilities, and underground transit routes. Dead soldiers left behind logs asking the same question: why did help never arrive? The records reveal that the outbreak was allowed to spread inside the sector as part of an experiment.

ACT 03

The Revelation

You reach the central communications tower and restore access to the complete Directive I file. The truth is worse than the outbreak itself: Directive I was never a rescue mission.

A.P.E.X / Directive File Recovered

Sanitization Protocol.

Directive I is a total-cleansing order. Your original mission was to ensure that no civilian and no ordinary soldier escaped Sector 4 alive, preventing the biological weapons program from ever reaching the outside world.

Season I / Final Transmission

The agent breaks the directive.

After learning the truth, you turn against the organization that created you. The Directive I signal is cancelled, giving the remaining survivors a chance to escape the sector.

Live transmission // Human operator detected

“Directive I signal failure confirmed. Rogue agent detected. Initiate Directive II: Execution.”
